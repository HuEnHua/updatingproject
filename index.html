Before you begin

Consent to take part

This is an academic study about how people combine advice from more than one source. It runs at the Behavioural Research Laboratory, HKU Business School, and takes about 75 minutes.

What you will do. You will look at photographs of people and, for each one, say how likely you think it is that the person is older than 21. Two artificial-intelligence systems were shown the same photographs beforehand, and you will see what each of them answered.

Payment. You receive HK$60 for taking part. One of your judgements is also drawn at random at the end of the session and decides a prize of HK$200; the closer that judgement is to the truth, the better your chance of winning it.

No deception. Nothing in this study is staged. The photographs are of real people whose ages are on record, the two systems are real systems, and the answers you see are the answers they actually gave.

Risks and voluntariness. This is a minimal-risk decision study. Taking part is voluntary and you may stop at any point by telling the experimenter; you will still receive the HK$60.

Your data. We record your judgements, your response times, and the session and seat you were assigned. We do not record your name or any other identifying information. Data are stored securely and reported in aggregate.

By continuing you confirm that you are 18 or older, that you have read the above, and that you agree to take part.
